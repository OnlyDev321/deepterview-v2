## 📖 Overview

Deepterview is an AI-powered mock interview platform designed to simulate real interview environments and provide comprehensive feedback beyond traditional text-based evaluations.

Unlike conventional interview preparation services that rely on predefined questions and post-interview analysis, Deepterview delivers:

- 🎤 Speech-to-Text (STT) based answer analysis
- 😊 Real-time facial expression and confidence tracking
- 👀 Eye contact and non-verbal behavior monitoring
- 🤖 Personalized interview questions generated by LLMs
- 📄 Resume and portfolio-based interview simulation
- ⭐ STAR methodology coaching
- 📊 Comprehensive interview reports and improvement guidance

The platform aims to help university students, job seekers, and professionals prepare for interviews more effectively through AI-driven personalized coaching.
